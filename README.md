# Allison Cairns

**Ph.D. Candidate in Applied Physics | Yale University**

**Advanced Optical Microscopy · Quantitative Neuroimaging · Connectomics**

I am a doctoral researcher in the [Kuan Lab](https://github.com/kuan-lab) and [Bewersdorf Lab](https://github.com/bewersdorflab) at Yale University, working at the intersection of optical microscopy, computational image analysis, and neuroscience.

My research leverages **pan-Expansion Microscopy (pan-ExM)** to investigate nanoscale synaptic architecture and molecular organization in neurodevelopmental disorders, with a particular focus on **Shank2-associated autism spectrum disorder**.

### Current Research

**Quantitative Characterization of Synaptic Organization in Shank2 Knockout Models**

Using approximately 24× tissue expansion and ~15 nm effective spatial resolution, my work aims to quantify alterations in synaptic ultrastructure and protein organization across wild-type and Shank2 knockout mouse brain tissue.

Key analytical objectives include:

- **3D Synaptic Morphometry:** Quantification of synaptic dimensions, morphology, and structural variability.
- **Molecular Spatial Organization:** Analysis of postsynaptic protein distributions, nanoscale clustering, and relative molecular localization.
- **Pre-/Postsynaptic Architecture:** Quantification of molecular alignment, spatial relationships, and synaptic organization.
- **Genotype-Dependent Differences:** Statistical comparison of synaptic structural and molecular phenotypes between wild-type and Shank2 knockout models.
- **Computational Image Analysis:** Development and application of reproducible workflows for 3D segmentation, quantitative fluorescence analysis, and spatial statistics.
